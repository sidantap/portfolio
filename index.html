        data engineering · runs nightly
        
Nightly 311 pipeline

        
Every night it pulls Cincinnati 311 requests, NOAA weather and NHTSA vehicle complaints, lands them unchanged, then cleans, tests and models them with dbt. Bad rows go to quarantine with a reason, and an open-source model classifies each new complaint.
