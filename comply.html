The dot is the mascot

The period in “comply.” is the firefly’s lantern. The intro crashes the word into the dot, the firefly circles, lights it, lands in front of the name, and the whole logo docks into the nav.

Flight is simulated, not keyframed

The firefly steers toward a target with arrival braking and a slow wander, pitches with its speed, beats its wings in flight and folds them when it lands. Its light flashes on its own and brightens when you scroll or come close.

It doubles as the demo guide

In the product demo it flies to the next button and says what to press. Plain SVG, CSS and JavaScript, no libraries, and it holds still for visitors who turn motion off.
